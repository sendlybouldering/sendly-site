Sendly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Sendly is a bouldering app that scans climbing walls, finds the routes and tracks your climbs. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and how to delete it. We wrote it in plain language.

Who we are: Sendly is run by Assaf Dotan, based in Israel. Assaf Dotan is the "data controller", meaning the one responsible for your data. Contact: sendlybouldering@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect your account details, your wall photos and what Sendly finds on them, and your climbs (gyms, sessions, tries, sends, XP). We use them to run the app for you and to make Sendly's scanning better.
  • Wall photos are used to train Sendly's models. This is on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Location is used only with your permission, only while you use the app, to find gyms near you. You can say no and still use Sendly.
  • Sendly doesn't show ads.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • Your data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). You can delete your account and data from inside the app. Wall photos you shared for training stay in Sendly's training set, with nothing linking them to you.

Your wall photos and model training

Training is on by default: we use your wall photos, hold outlines, colors and corrections, together with the gym they were taken at, to improve how Sendly detects holds and colors.

  • People are blurred before upload. While training is on, before a photo leaves your phone, Sendly looks for people in it and blurs them in the uploaded copy. If that check can't run, the photo isn't used for training. Photos uploaded while training is off are only kept in your account (not blurred, never used for training).
  • Scans you don't save may also be uploaded for training while training is on: over Wi-Fi only (never mobile data, a hotspot or Low Data Mode), at most 20 a day, and only after the people check ran, with the same blurring.
  • Turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy → Share wall photos for training. From then on, new photos aren't used, and photos you already shared are left out of future training. They stay in your account so you can still see your routes.
  • If you delete your account, the wall photos you shared for training (with people blurred) stay in Sendly's training set, together with what Sendly found on them (hold outlines, colors, your corrections, grade, the public gym). Everything that links them to you is removed: your account, user ID, name and route names. Photos you didn't share for training are deleted.
  • If Sendly later adds climbing videos, the same rules apply to them.

Sendly's trained models are Sendly's own. They learn general patterns (what a hold looks like), and nobody can rebuild your photos from them.

Location

Sendly asks for your location only with your permission (the iPhone permission prompt), and only while you use the app. Sendly never uses your location in the background.

  • Allow: Sendly finds climbing gyms near you and suggests the gym you're at. Your location itself isn't stored; only the gyms you pick are saved in your account.
  • Don't allow: you pick gyms by hand. Everything else still works.

Gym searches go through Apple Maps, so Apple's privacy policy applies to those searches. You can turn location off any time in your iPhone's Settings → Sendly → Location.

We never save the location stored inside your photos (photo GPS).

When you pick a gym that Apple Maps found, its public details (name, address, map position) join a shared gym list every Sendly user can see. Gyms you add by hand (for example, a home wall) stay private to you.

Ads

Sendly doesn't show ads today. If we add ads in the future, we'll update this policy first, and it will work like this:

  • Personalized ads: with your permission, we and our ad partners use your advertising ID and what we know from your use of Sendly (like your level and the gyms you visit) to show ads that fit you. On iPhone, Apple asks you first ("Allow Sendly to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites?"). In the EU, UK and Switzerland, we also show a consent screen before any personalized ads.
  • Without permission, you may still see ads, but they aren't personalized and we don't use your advertising ID.
  • Change your mind any time: an Ad choices setting in Sendly, and iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

We'll list our ad partners here before ads go live.

Who we share data with

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with services that help run Sendly, under contracts that protect it:

  • Supabase (database, login and file storage, EU servers in Frankfurt)
  • Google and Apple (only if you sign in with them; Apple Maps for gym search)
  • Apple also handles the crash reports and app analytics you choose to share with developers (see the table above)

We may also share data if the law requires it, or with a buyer if Sendly is ever sold (this policy would still apply).

If any of these providers handle data outside the EU, they use legal safeguards such as the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Delete your account and data

In Sendly, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, climbs and everything linked to you from Sendly's servers. Wall photos you shared for training stay, anonymously, in Sendly's training set; all your other photos and scans are deleted. It can't be undone. Climbs saved on your phone stay there until you delete the app. You can also email sendlybouldering@gmail.com and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.

Guests (no account): go to Settings → Account → Delete my data. It deletes your guest data from Sendly's servers and from your phone, with the same rule for training photos. You can also email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR in the EU and UK), you can ask us to:

  • see a copy of your data, or get it in a file you can take elsewhere
  • fix wrong data
  • delete your data
  • stop or limit some uses (for example, training)
  • withdraw consent (location, sharing analytics with developers) at any time

Email sendlybouldering@gmail.com. We'll answer within 30 days. To get a copy of your data, email us from the address on your account (guests: first create an account in Settings → Account, which keeps your climbs, so we can find your data) and we'll send it as a file you can open or move elsewhere. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority; in the EU or UK, your local data protection authority.

Children

Sendly is for people aged 13 and older (16 in EU countries where that's the age of digital consent, unless a parent agrees). If Sendly ever shows ads, it won't show personalized ads to anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and Supabase's security protections. No system is perfect, but we work to keep your data safe.

Changes

If we change this policy in a big way, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
